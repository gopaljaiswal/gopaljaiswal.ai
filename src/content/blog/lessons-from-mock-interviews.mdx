After running a number of mock interviews, the same feedback keeps
recurring — independent of seniority level.

## 1. You are narrating too late

Interviewers can't grade thinking they can't see. Say what you're about to
try, and why, before you try it.

## 2. You are optimizing for "correct" over "clear"

A design that is 90% right and clearly explained usually scores higher than
one that's 100% right but delivered as a wall of jargon.

## 3. You are not asking about constraints you'll need later

Requirements gathering isn't a formality — the constraints you skip early
tend to resurface as surprises halfway through the design.
