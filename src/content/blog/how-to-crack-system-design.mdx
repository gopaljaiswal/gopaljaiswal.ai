Most system design prep optimizes for the wrong thing: memorizing a handful of
"standard" architectures (a URL shortener, a chat app, a feed) and hoping the
interview question is close enough to one of them.

What actually gets evaluated is whether you have a repeatable way of thinking
about *any* system — how you gather requirements, how you reason about
trade-offs, and how you communicate a design as it evolves under new
constraints.

## A rough shape for the conversation

1. Clarify the requirements and scale before drawing a single box.
2. Start with the simplest design that could plausibly work.
3. Introduce complexity only in response to a constraint you've named out loud.
4. Narrate trade-offs, not just choices — say what you're giving up.
